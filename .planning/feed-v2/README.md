# Feed v2 — the card formats

A discussion round, written down. **Nothing here has been built or approved for
building.** It is the record of a conversation on 2026-09-29 about what is wrong
with the Feed's card model and what would replace it.

Read in this order:

1. **[DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)** — what is wrong, in Aman's words, and what
   was settled. Start here; the catalogue makes no sense without it.
2. **[CATALOGUE.md](CATALOGUE.md)** — the 29 question archetypes, the 8
   interaction primitives that carry them, and the 3 answer shapes they store as.
3. **[OPEN-QUESTIONS.md](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)** — what was deliberately not
   settled. Silence in the other two files is not agreement; it is this file.

## The short version

The Feed asks you to type paragraphs. Nobody will, so half the corpus — 1,383
of 2,808 cards — is a format that will not be used, and it is the half that
costs money, because a typed answer is graded by a model.

The replacement is a wider catalogue of formats you answer by tapping, all of
them graded deterministically. Typing stays in mocks and the Coach, where you
have sat down on purpose.

Two things fall out of that: the Feed's running cost goes to zero, and the
1,383 typed cards need regenerating.
