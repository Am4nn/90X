How a lesson works Coach teaches the pattern from grounded sources, works an example with you, then asks follow-ups.

Direction A. Weakest patterns first with one “Teach me” action each; the full pattern index below; a pointer to non-DSA topic lessons in the Library. Fastest route from “I'm bad at X” to “teach me X”.

Direction B

Every lesson, grouped by area

A browsable catalog of all authored lessons across every area, each opening the lesson and offering “Teach me”. Trade: mirrors the Library's breadth and is the most complete; it is a catalog to scan, not a “what should I do” answer.
