← All screens

Three directions for putting the Sunday weekly review — the coach's own score, the written read, and the suggested plan changes — in front of you on Today. The day line, the 90 grid, the readiness strip and the missions are unchanged; only the coach's read is added or expanded.

Direction A · the roll leads

Full card, at the top of the day

The weekly read is promoted to a first-class card above the missions, so a fresh read is the first thing you see. Trade: pushes the day's work down one screen; only worth it while the read is fresh.
